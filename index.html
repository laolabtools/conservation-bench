    Export

    Alignment: progressive profile alignment (BLOSUM62, affine gaps, ClustalW-style sequence weights), then a guide tree re-estimated from the first alignment and tree-split iterative refinement on a sum-of-pairs score. On BAliBASE families (BALIFAM benchmark) it recovers about 88% of reference residue pairs. Very long or repetitive regions (e.g. the RPB1 CTD) remain hard for any aligner.
